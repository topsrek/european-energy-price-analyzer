# EEPA Roadmap & Vision

This document outlines the long-term vision and potential features for the European Energy Price Analyzer (EEPA).

## 1. Custom Dashboard
*   **Concept:** A highly customizable landing page or sub-page where users can build their own view.
*   **Features:**
    *   **Movable/Resizable Cards:** Drag-and-drop interface to arrange widgets.
    *   **Widget Library:** A vast collection of cards to choose from (e.g., specific region spot prices, network cost breakdowns, historical averages).
    *   **Persistence:** Save dashboard configurations to local storage or user profiles.
*   **Status:** Potential future project or sub-page.

## 2. Price Prediction & Analysis
*   **Concept:** Move from purely historical data to future-looking insights.
*   **Features:**
    *   **Regression Models:** Predict prices for the next 24-48 hours or up to a year using different time scales.
    *   **Advanced Calendar Analysis:** Extra analysis for variable holidays (Easter, etc.) and calendar weeks.
    *   **Weather Integration:** Incorporate wind and solar forecasts, as they heavily influence spot prices.
*   **Status:** Proposed enhancement.

## 3. Automatic Reports & Calculations
*   **Concept:** Pre-generated or on-demand reports for specific use cases.
*   **Features:**
    *   **Monthly Savings Report:** How much could have been saved with a flexible tariff vs. a fixed one.
    *   **Appliance Planning:** Suggestions on when to run heavy appliances (washing machine, EV charging) based on tomorrow's prices.
    *   **Regional Benchmarking:** Deep-dive reports comparing market performance across Europe.
*   **Status:** Proposed as a sub-page feature.

## 4. Smart Meter Integration (V2)
*   **Concept:** Seamless integration of personal consumption data.
*   **Features:**
    *   **API Connectors:** Direct connection to grid provider portals (where APIs exist).
    *   **Demo Mode:** A pre-loaded dataset to show users the value of the analysis without needing their own file yet.
*   **Status:** Demo mode implemented (hidden); real upload pending.

## 5. Educational Onboarding
*   **Concept:** Helping users understand the complex energy market.
*   **Features:**
    *   **Hyperfloater Focus:** Explain that these are spot prices specifically relevant for "Hyperfloater" style tariffs.
    *   **Market Context:** Provide info on what percentage of the market uses these tariffs.
    *   **Feed-in Relevance:** Information for "Einspeiser" (producers) regarding their spot-based vs. fixed conditions.
*   **Status:** Implementation in progress.
